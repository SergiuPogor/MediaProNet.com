## What is MEDIAPRONET?

[MEDIAPRONET](https://mediapronet.com/) is a launch and discovery directory where builders list what they just shipped. Visitors browse newly launched startups, SaaS tools and online products by category, and each listing gets its own page with a link to the product.

## Who it is for

- **Indie hackers and small teams** who want their launch to be found by early adopters and by search engines.
- **Early adopters** looking for new tools, sorted by category.

## How listing works

1. Submit your website on [mediapronet.com](https://mediapronet.com/).
2. The listing is reviewed.
3. Approved products get their own listing page with a link to the product.

Makers can submit for free or pick a paid upgrade for faster publishing.
